How this is calculated
  • F&O profit is non-speculative business income. It is added to your salary and taxed at your slab rate, not at a flat capital-gains rate.
  • Tax on F&O = tax on (salary + F&O) − tax on salary alone. So you see exactly the extra tax your trading creates.
  • Includes 4% health & education cess, Section 87A rebate, and surcharge (with marginal relief) once total income crosses ₹50 lakh.
  • FY 2026-27 new regime slabs: 0–4L nil · 4–8L 5% · 8–12L 10% · 12–16L 15% · 16–20L 20% · 20–24L 25% · above 24L 30%. Standard deduction ₹75,000 (₹50,000 in old regime).
  • Enter net profit after brokerage and exchange charges. A net F&O loss for the year can't reduce tax on your salary, so tax never goes below what you'd pay on salary alone.
  • Data is stored only in this browser. This is an estimate, not tax advice; check with a CA before filing (F&O is reported in ITR-3, and advance tax may be due).
